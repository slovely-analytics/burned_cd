# Last Survivor NFL Pool Rules

Source context extracted from the attached DOCX.

## Rules

- $10 one-time entry fee.
- Splash charges a small fee of $1 per entry, deducted from the pool of money.
- Pick one NFL winning team per week, with no points involved.
- Ties lose.
- For Weeks 17 and 18, you must pick 2 games in each week to help reduce ties.
- You cannot use the same team twice.
- If you do not make a pick, Splash will assign a default pick, believed to be your highest-spread available team. This is not guaranteed, so do not rely on it.
- The pool ends when one person remains or when the NFL regular season ends, whichever comes first.
- If multiple players are still alive at season end, the money is divided evenly among survivors.
- You may enter as often as you want, up to 25 entries maximum.
- Picks are due by 1:00 PM Sunday, even if you are playing a later game.
- Thursday games are eligible too.
- If a game is cancelled or postponed to a different week and you selected a team in that game, you will be assigned the highest favorite still available to you.
